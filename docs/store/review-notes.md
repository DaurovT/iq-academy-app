**What the app is**
PharmIQ Academy is a professional learning and loyalty app for pharmacy staff and doctors in Uzbekistan
who take part in partner programs of pharmaceutical companies. Users take short courses and quizzes,
answer surveys, complete activity quests and earn IQC points.

**IQC points**
IQC are internal loyalty points of the program. They cannot be purchased (there are no in-app purchases
and no payments of any kind in the app) and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. Points are earned
only by completing courses, surveys and verified quests, and can be redeemed for partner gift vouchers.

**Quests**
- Pharmacists upload a photo of a pharmacy receipt. An AI model reads the product names and a moderator
  verifies the submission; verified receipts count toward the quest goal.
- Doctors upload a photo of a prescription form (“blank”) they issued. The AI extracts the medicine names;
  verified forms count toward the quest goal.
- Medical representatives see aggregated activity of the pharmacists linked to them.

**Super Sapper (mini-app) — loyalty points prize promotion**
A scheduled prize promotion for program participants. No money is involved anywhere: participants pick cells
on a shared field using IQC loyalty points, which are earned only through learning, surveys and verified quests
and cannot be purchased, transferred or exchanged for money (the app has no payments or in-app purchases).
The full prize list, cell price and dates are shown before participation; all cells are revealed at the announced
time and prizes (partner gift vouchers or bonus points) are delivered automatically; if a promotion is cancelled,
all points are refunded. The organizer is PHARMIQ ACADEMY LLC; Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved.
Official rules are in the app — Home → Mini apps → Super Sapper → “Promotion rules” (also linked from the cell
confirmation dialog) — and at https://pharmview.uz/legal/sapper-rules

**Sign-in**
Phone number with an SMS code, Sign in with Apple, Google, or Telegram. On first sign-in with Apple/Google
the user confirms a phone number once, because the program account is identified by phone.

**Account deletion**
Profile → “Delete account”. Personal data (profile, phone number, sign-in links, notifications, support chat)
is deleted immediately; anonymized accrual and voucher records are kept for accounting. Sign in with Apple
access is revoked on deletion. Web instructions: https://pharmview.uz/delete-account
